03 Domain Model

Every idea is a state machine

Six states, five transitions — verified directly in PermissionValidator, not assumed from the feature list.

Draft owner's eyes only
Published visible to all
Review verdict pending
Approved green-lit
Progress in build
Archived aged out, nightly job
Rejected the other verdict from Review — never both
owner-only

A Draft is invisible to everyone but its owner — including every historical revision. No role bypasses this, not even Admin — verified in PermissionValidator, zero exceptions in the code.

votable window

Liking only works on Published, Review, Approved, Progress. Once Rejected or Archived, an idea stays exactly as visible as before — it just stops accepting votes.

automatic

Nobody clicks "archive." A nightly job at 00:00 UTC retires overdue Progress ideas, paged 500 at a time, retried up to 5× with backoff.

06 Architecture

One repo, two independent apps

Merged from two previously separate repositories — kept deliberately independent, not unified.

innovator/ two repos, merged — still two apps
apps/frontend Standalone Angular, no NgModules, feature-based structure
frontend-*.yml · paths: apps/frontend/**
apps/backend Spring Boot API, own dependencies & versioning
backend-*.yml · paths: apps/backend/**
./run <action> <app> — a dispatcher, not a build system. No unifying toolchain on top, by design.

servicesbusiness rules
repositoryssic, deliberateSpring Data JPA
